A little help, right here

Something look different?

Stop at the step that is blocked. Do not switch tenants, weaken security settings, or purchase anything to work around an error.

I cannot sign in, or the wrong organisation appears

Use the work account confirmed by your IT team. Check the account shown in Copilot Studio. If the browser selected another account, use your organisation's approved browser profile or sign out of Copilot Studio and choose the correct account. Complete your normal MFA. An event-provided account must come from your organiser; do not assume a guest invitation grants authoring access. [6]

My environment is missing, or agent creation is disabled

Ask IT to confirm the exact environment name, Dataverse database, supported region, your environment access and authoring permissions. The environment selector can appear in the top bar or the left navigation as the UI changes. Do not create a new environment yourself unless IT explicitly instructs you. [5][8]

I cannot find New experience or the New agent menu

Go to Home and look for New experience; when shown, leave it ON. Then select Agents and the arrow next to New agent. Some rollouts already use the new shell and do not show the same toggle. If the screen does not match, ask your facilitator to confirm the right route. Do not use a preview URL or turn the toggle off just to match an older article. [14][UI]

It says I need credits, a licence, consent, or permission

Stop before accepting a purchase, trial, consent or billing change. Send the message to your IT contact. For the GitHub Copilot harness, building and testing use Copilot Credits; an M365 Copilot licence does not cover that usage. Credit or policy errors belong to IT, not to the beginner exercise. [3][4]

The agent replies differently, or does not reply

Check the selected harness, confirm you saved the instructions, and start a fresh test chat. Send Ready check once more. Minor wording differences do not prove an access failure. If the response is unrelated, use the help request below; do not mark the test complete. For a permission, billing or policy error, stop rather than repeatedly retrying. [11][13]

I need to request access or report a blocker

Copy this into your company's approved IT request channel and fill in the brackets. It is not sent automatically. Never include passwords, tokens, MFA codes or real customer/patient data.

    UI evidence and event-specific guidance
    
        
    The New agent dropdown illustration is cropped from an organiser-provided screenshot received on 17 September 2026. It shows both choices while New experience is on. It supports menu labels, not licensing claims. The signed-in interface was also inspected on that date; navigation placement differs by rollout.
    
        
    Some public Standard instructions still start by turning New experience off. This guide intentionally keeps it on and uses the organiser's illustrated dropdown; see reference 14 for other supported access routes. If a customer does not have this menu, their facilitator must confirm the route.
    
        
    The company-selected harness, readiness checkpoints, sample agent name and test prompt are guide design choices. They are not Microsoft licensing requirements. Event date, venue, deadlines, support contacts, approved recipes and any supplied-tenant terms must come from the organiser's current event pack.
    
      
    
      
    Before distributing for another event, the owner should re-open the billing, access and policy references, compare the UI with the intended customer rollout, and confirm the final track dependencies. Do not treat an unanswered track requirement as “not applicable.”
